Our Story

About Amantran Guest House

45+ years of warm hospitality, authentic food, and memorable stays in the heart of Tarapith.

🏨

A Legacy of Hospitality Since 1980

Amantran Guest House was founded over 45 years ago by Raja Babu with a simple mission — to provide every traveller and pilgrim visiting Tarapith with a home-like experience filled with warmth, great food, and comfortable stays.

What started as a small eatery in Belia has grown into the most recognized hospitality establishment in the Tarapith-Rampurhat region. Today, Amantran is synonymous with trust, quality, and genuine care.

Locals and visitors alike know that when they step into Amantran, they'll be greeted with a smile, served delicious food prepared with fresh ingredients, and given a clean, comfortable room. Our bar lounge offers a relaxing environment to unwind after a long journey.

We take pride in being deeply connected to our community. Many of our staff have been with us for decades, and several families have been returning guests for generations. That's the kind of trust we've built — not through advertising, but through genuine service.

Meet the Founder

Raja Babu — The Heart of Amantran

👨‍💼

A Man of Vision & Values

Raja Babu started Amantran Guest House with just a small kitchen and a dream to serve the best food to pilgrims visiting the sacred Tarapith Temple. His philosophy has always been simple: treat every guest like family.

Over four decades, he has personally ensured that every dish meets his exacting standards, every room is spotless, and every guest leaves with a smile. His hands-on approach and warm personality have made Amantran not just a business, but an institution in Birbhum.

Today, Raja Babu continues to greet guests personally, supervise the kitchen, and maintain the same high standards that made Amantran a household name in Tarapith and Rampurhat.

"Every guest is a member of our family. That's not just a saying — it's how we run Amantran."
